How do I pay my semester fee?
9:41 AM
JD

You can pay your semester fee through the university portal at portal.university.edu. Here's a quick guide:

1. Log in with your student credentials
2. Navigate to Finances → Semester Fee
3. Select your payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or instalments)
4. Download the receipt after payment

The deadline for this semester is March 31, 2026. Late payments incur a 5% surcharge.
